01 — About

Two disciplines,
one visual language.

Based in Sri Lanka. Working in Sinhala and English.

2025Digital Marketing Executive
32Videos published
4Qualifications

I'm a fourth-year Mass Communication undergraduate at the University of Kelaniya with an interest in content writing, graphic design and video editing. I continuously develop my skills through learning and practical experience, and I'm working towards a career in digital marketing.

As a graphic designer and video editor I work across the two halves of visual communication: the still frame that has to stop a scroll, and the moving one that has to hold attention for two minutes.

My design work centres on poster and campaign design — festival identities, tuition-class advertising, product creative and social graphics — built in Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator. My video work covers editing, short-form effects and cinematography, from a documentary piece for the University of Kelaniya's Mass Communication department to a dance-cover film shot on a phone and cut into something that has travelled far beyond its source.

"During the COVID-19 pandemic, I began experimenting with video editing, which significantly expanded my knowledge and skills in the field."

That experiment became a channel. Click On Magic — 32 videos of practical magic and illusion built in-camera and finished in the edit — is where the motion side of this practice was learned, one cut at a time.
